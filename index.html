Concurrent Exercise and Learning

Participate in a two-day research study examining how exercise-induced stress may affect learning.

Project number18-S26-CON
SemesterSummer 2026
Faculty sponsorChristopher Conway
Student researcherTanadanai Hawsatitam

What to expect

***Please wear athletic/comfortable clothes***

The study takes place on two consecutive days. On Day 1, you will rate your overall fitness level and may be asked to complete two separate 10-minute biking sessions while attending to flashing stimuli on a computer screen.

An armband will measure your heart rate throughout the process. You will also provide saliva samples at three separate time points during the experiment.

If participants have sensitivities to flashing visuals, or have visual impairments, they should not participate in this experiment. Individuals with medical conditions that prohibit moderate exercise (e.g., cardiovascular or respiratory conditions), current injuries that prevent cycling, or use of medications known to substantially affect cortisol should not participate.

Please complete both study days so your data can be included in the analysis.
